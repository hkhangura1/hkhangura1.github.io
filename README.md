Final Project Description:

For my final project, I decided to simulate Maxwell's equation of a electric field around a point charge. This project mainly focuses on using particle systems. The way it works is the particles will generate on screen and then move off screen to a location such as (-999,-999). Then, when you click down on the mouse and hold, a charged particle and should appear and there should be an eletric field (more particles) outputting from the charged particle. When you move the mouse around on the screen, you should also be able to see the particle moving. Along with it the electric field will also move and adjust the field. So on screen it should seem like it is snaping back into place!
